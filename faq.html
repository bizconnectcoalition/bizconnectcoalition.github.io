Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions.

The short version: it's free to attend, it's built on communities you already belong to, and the facilitator seat comes with real upside. The longer version is below.

What exactly is Biz Connect Coalition?
A business-networking organization whose chapters are each built around a shared commonality — a gym, a school's alumni, a veterans' organization, a congregation, a club. Because members already share something real, networking is warmer and easier than a generic room of strangers. Our mission: through an integrative business development ecosystem, we provide an incubator for small business expansion, innovation and creation.
How much does it cost to attend?
Nothing. Core membership is free forever and includes the monthly in-person chapter events. Paid tiers exist for members who want more — a directory listing, attendee contact lists, and referral tools — starting at $195/year for Premium.
What makes this different from BNI or LeTip?
Three things. First, the commonality: our chapters are built on existing communities, so trust exists before the first meeting. Second, the price: attending is free, and our invitation-only Circles run a fraction of the cost of comparable groups. Third, the model: facilitators share in the revenue their chapter generates rather than paying franchise fees.
What is a chapter family?
The flavor of commonality a chapter is built on. Member Biz Connect (gyms, clubs, associations), Alumni Biz Connect (schools and universities), Veteran Biz Connect (those who served), Heroes Biz Connect (first responders), and Circle Biz Connect (invitation-only referral circles, one seat per industry).
What does a facilitator actually do?
Host one evening a month and be the connector you probably already are. The Coalition provides the chapter page, member directory, admin tools, training, and support free of charge — and facilitators receive free Premium membership plus revenue share on member upgrades. Details on the facilitator page.
How do I start a chapter?
Fill out the charter application — it takes about three minutes. We'll reach out to schedule a launch call, confirm your commonality and venue, and get your chapter onto the map. Most chapters can go from idea to first event in a few weeks.
What are Circles?
Invitation-only referral groups with one member per industry — your seat locks out your competitors. Circles require a minimum of 10 members to open, meet on a focused cadence, and cost $399/year: a friendlier, more affordable alternative to traditional referral organizations.
What do Premium members get for $195/year?
Your business listed in your chapter's premium member directory, the attendee contact list after every event, the member dashboard with referral tracking, and the chapter's virtual community. Core members can see the directory; Premium members are in it.
My community isn't listed. Can I still join?
That's the best position to be in. Open territory means first-mover advantage — start the chapter yourself and claim the facilitator seat, or contact us and we'll help you find the nearest room.
When does the member portal launch?
The member platform — profiles, dashboards, event registration, and referral tracking — is being integrated with this site now. See a preview on the member portal page.
